IMAGE CREDITS - Saltline Retreats
All photographs are downloaded and served locally from assets/images/.
Nothing is hotlinked. No watermarks, no brand logos, no identifiable
individuals (every person in this set is a distant silhouette), no minors.

NOTE ON TREATMENT: every photograph on this site is duotoned in CSS at
render time using mix-blend-mode - shadows resolve to deep sea #0C3B4A and
highlights to sand #E8DCC8. The files below are unmodified colour originals
apart from resizing and compression; the two-colour look is the stylesheet.
